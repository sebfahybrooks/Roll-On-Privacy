Privacy Policy for Roll On

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Roll On is a football team-management app for grassroots coaches in the UK, published by Seb Fahy-Brooks, contactable at pushpull@fahybrooks.co.uk. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how you can control it. It's written for coaches using the app and for parents/guardians of players whose details a coach has added.

Roll On is only distributed in the UK and is intended for use by adult coaches and team managers. It is not directed at children, and children do not use the app themselves.

What information Roll On stores

When a coach uses Roll On to manage a team, the app stores:

  • Player details: name, squad number, preferred position, an optional photo, activity status, and match history (minutes played, goals, assists, player-of-the-week, availability for fixtures).
  • Coach/manager details: your name or display name, and your role on each team you're part of.
  • Team details: team name, colour, an optional club badge/logo, and squad size.
  • Fixtures and events: opponents, dates, locations, formations, lineups, substitutions, and notes you add.

Roll On does not ask for or store financial information, government ID numbers, health/medical records, or precise location data. It does not include advertising, and it does not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

Where this information is stored

All of the information above is stored in a private database in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit service (NSPersistentCloudKitContainer). This is how the app keeps your data in sync across your own devices and lets it survive a phone upgrade.

  • By default, this data sits in your private CloudKit database — Apple encrypts it in transit and at rest, and neither Apple nor the developer of Roll On can read its contents for their own purposes.
  • If you use team sharing to invite another coach or manager to a team (via an invite code), the records for that team — including player names and details — become visible to that person too, through Apple's CKShare mechanism. Only people you explicitly invite, and who accept the invite, gain access.
  • A small "next event" summary (team name, colour, opponent, date, and a resized team crest) is also stored locally on your device in a shared App Group container, so the home-screen widget can display it without opening the app. This never leaves your device.
  • The developer of Roll On does not operate a server, does not receive a copy of your data, and cannot access what's stored in your iCloud account.

Who can see a player's information

  • The coach/manager who added the player.
  • Any other coach or manager who has been invited to that specific team and accepted the invite.
  • Nobody else. Roll On does not publish, sell, or share player or coach data with any third party, advertiser, or analytics provider.

Your rights and how to exercise them

Because this data lives in your own iCloud account, you're in direct control of it at all times:

  • View or correct a player's details at any time from their profile in the Squad screen.
  • Delete an individual player from Player Details → Delete Player. This permanently removes their profile and match history from your device and your iCloud account.
  • Delete an entire team, including every player, fixture, and match history, from that team's "Manage Team" screen → Danger Zone → Delete Team. You can also selectively clear just the squad, fixtures, or events from the same screen.
  • Remove a coach's access to a team from "Manage Team", which stops them seeing that team's data going forward.
  • Stop sharing entirely by turning off iCloud for Roll On in your device's Settings app, or by signing out of iCloud, though this also stops your data syncing across your own devices.

If you're a parent or guardian and have a question about how a club or coach is using Roll On to store your child's details, the data controller is the coach or club who entered that information, not the Roll On developer — they're best placed to action a correction or deletion request. If you need help reaching them or have a question about the app itself, contact pushpull@fahybrooks.co.uk.

Data retention

Information stays in your iCloud account for as long as you keep it in the app. There's no separate backend copy and no automatic deletion schedule — deleting a player or team in the app deletes it from iCloud too, on your next sync.

Children's information

Roll On is used by adult coaches to record details of youth players for team-management purposes (lineups, substitutions, playing time). We recommend clubs and coaches using the app for youth teams do so under their existing safeguarding and parental-consent arrangements (for example, an FA-affiliated club's standard registration consent), and only enter the minimum information needed to run training and matches.
